# Is Fomo App Safe? What to Check Before You Sign Up With Code DEAL (2026)

If you plan to join Fomo with referral code DEAL through the invite link fomo.family/r/deal, run through this safety checklist first. Fomo is a real app from a real company, FOMO Labs, Inc. Its strong points: it does not hold your funds, and it shows its fee before every trade. Its weak points: no regulator licenses it, trades can't be undone, and many tokens on it can crash or become impossible to sell.

Last checked: 20 October 2026

## Who holds your money?

You do. The wallet is non-custodial and built with Privy. Its private key is split into several shares, so no single party, Fomo included, can rebuild it alone. You can export it at any time.

There is no undo button: a confirmed blockchain trade or transfer is final, with no refund if a token drops. ([How Fomo keeps your crypto secure](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14435103-how-fomo-keeps-your-crypto-secure))

## What protects your account?

With no seed phrase, the Apple, Google or email account you sign in with is the real lock on your wallet. If someone takes it over, your funds are at risk.

- Turn on two-factor login for that account.
- Turn on Fomo's biometric lock, so withdrawals and key exports need Face ID, Touch ID or a fingerprint.
- Type fomo.family yourself. Scam sites use lookalike addresses.
- Never share your private key. Fomo says it will never ask for it.

## Will you see the fee before you confirm?

Yes. The fee summary shows Fomo's fee, as a rate or as the minimum that applies, and a second line for the network fee. The standard spot fee is 0.50% per trade, or 0.45% with any referral code. Fomo pays gas on Solana only; elsewhere, network fees come on top. Watch two extras: small Solana trades have minimum fees, and some tokens carry a contract fee set by their creator. ([Trading fees on Fomo](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14436214-trading-fees-on-fomo))

## Which tokens need extra care?

A blue checkmark means Fomo's team reviewed a token. It is not a promise about price or safety. Unreviewed tokens show a "be cautious" popup, and tokens with signs of fraud get a warning label. A token may also be impossible to sell if the liquidity was pulled or the creator blocked sales. Tokens that land in your wallet without a purchase are almost always scams. ([Protecting yourself from scams](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/14436622-protecting-yourself-from-scams))

## How risky are perps?

Leverage goes up to 40x on BTC and 10x on most altcoins, and a leveraged position can be closed automatically. Costs stack up: Fomo's 0.05% at open and at close, Hyperliquid's fee on the full leveraged position, and hourly funding. Perps are not open to U.S. persons, and a VPN does not change that. ([Leverage on perps](https://help.fomo.family/en/articles/15451940-leverage-on-perps))

## Is Fomo regulated?

No. Fomo's Terms of Service say no regulator has registered or licensed it, and that it is not an exchange or a broker.
