## Step 1: no account yet

- [ ] You are creating a new Fomo account. Existing accounts can't take a code.
- [ ] You open https://fomo.family/r/deal on the phone or computer you will sign up on.
- [ ] You have an Apple ID or a Google account ready. Fomo uses one of these to sign up, and there is no seed phrase to write down.

## Step 2: the sign-up itself

- [ ] You create the account from the invite link, not some other way. The code is applied on the sign-up screen.
- [ ] You don't plan to "add the code later". Fomo has no field for that.

Where the code box appears on an iPhone, an Android phone or the web app: [Fomo app referral code](https://fomoreferralcode.app/app-referral-code/).

## Step 3: money in, first trade

- [ ] You fund the account with USDC on Solana, Base, BNB Chain, Monad or Ethereum, or with USDG on Robinhood Chain. Whether card, Apple Pay or Pay with Coinbase shows up depends on where you live and on the account.
- [ ] On a regular-token spot trade of 190 USDC or more, the Fomo fee line shows 0.45%.
- [ ] On Base, BNB Chain, Monad, Ethereum, Robinhood Chain or Arc, a second line shows the network fee. On Solana, Fomo pays the gas.
- [ ] You count a buy and a later sell as two trades. Each one has its own fee.

## Step 4: when the fee line looks wrong

- [ ] The fee line shows 0.50% on a regular token: no referrer may have been recorded.
- [ ] You did come through the link: open Profile > Settings > Help and Support > Contact Support in the app and ask to be attached to DEAL.
- [ ] You signed up before opening the link: nothing can be changed. A recorded referrer can't be removed or swapped.
- [ ] A small Solana trade shows a flat amount instead of a percentage: that is Fomo's small-trade minimum, so check a trade of 190 USDC or more instead.

## Step 5: waiting for the USDC

- [ ] Nothing to claim. No form, no wallet address.
- [ ] USDC lands in your Fomo account, monthly, whatever the amount.
- [ ] Once 50 USDC or more is unpaid, payments move to weekly, at the weekend.
- [ ] The first monthly payout is due in the first week of November 2026.

## Not a bigger discount

Every Fomo referral code unlocks the same 10%. DEAL does not cut Fomo's fee any deeper. The cashback is a separate USDC payment, made by the code owner out of their own referral share.

## Who pays the DEAL cashback?

- The DEAL code owner pays it, not Fomo.
- It comes from half of the 25% referral share Fomo pays the code owner. That is 12.5% of your Fomo fees.
- It is paid monthly, or weekly at the weekend once 50 USDC is unpaid.
- The code owner sent seven $50.00 payouts to DEAL users on 20 September 2026 and seven on 17 October 2026.
- The first monthly cashback is set for the first week of November 2026.

## What the cashback leaves out

- Network fees on chains other than Solana.
- Hyperliquid's own perps fee and hourly funding.
- Any contract fee a token's creator has set.

Only Fomo's own fee, on spot and perps, feeds the cashback.

Meme coins swing hard, and money put into them can be lost.
