# Fomo Referral Code DEAL: Sign-Up Checklist and Fixes (2026)

The Fomo referral code is DEAL, and this checklist is about making it stick: open fomo.family/r/deal before your Fomo account exists and you get 10% Off + Fee Cashback.

The 10% off trading fees is Fomo's own referral discount, identical for every code. The fee cashback, 12.5% of the Fomo fees you pay in USDC, is added by the DEAL code owner. Fomo itself pays no cashback.

Last checked: 20 October 2026.

## Before you sign up

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| You don't have a Fomo account yet | A code is applied only when the account is created |
| You're on the device you'll sign up on | Open the invite on that same phone or computer |
| fomo.family/r/deal is open on that device | That link carries code DEAL |
| You know your sign-in: Apple, Google or email | Any of the three works; there is no seed phrase |

## During sign-up

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open fomo.family/r/deal, then install Fomo (iOS or Android) or use the web app at fomo.family |
| 2 | Create your account with Apple, Google or email |
| 3 | If a referral or invite code field appears, enter DEAL: four letters, no spaces |
| 4 | Finish signing up and add money (USDC on Solana, Base, BNB Chain, Monad or Ethereum; USDG on Robinhood Chain) |

Card, Apple Pay and Pay with Coinbase deposits depend on your country and account.

## After sign-up: did the code work?

| What to look at | What you should see |
|---|---|
| Fee summary on a spot trade of a regular token, 190 USDC or more | Fomo fee of 0.45%, not 0.50% |
| The second line of the fee summary | The network fee, shown separately (on Solana, Fomo pays it) |
| Your Fomo balance after the first payout date | DEAL cashback in USDC: monthly, or weekly at the weekend once 50 USDC is unpaid; first monthly payout in the first week of November 2026 |

## Problems and fixes

| What you see | Likely reason | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0.50% fee on a normal-size trade | No referrer was recorded at sign-up | In the app: Profile > Settings > Help and Support > Contact Support. Ask to be attached to DEAL |
| A high fee on a tiny Solana trade | Solana minimum fees: 0.10 USDC under 5 USDC, 2% from 5 to 47.50 USDC, 0.95 USDC from 47.50 to 190 USDC | Judge the fee on a trade of 190 USDC or more; this isn't a code problem |
| A 0.05% fee | Some major tokens have their own 0.05% Fomo fee | Normal; check a regular token instead |
| An extra charge on Base, BNB Chain, Monad, Ethereum, Robinhood Chain or Arc | The network fee, added on top of Fomo's fee | Expected; no code lowers network fees |
| A token-specific fee | Some tokens carry a contract fee set by their creator | Separate from Fomo's fee and from any code |
| You already had a Fomo account | Codes only apply at sign-up | A recorded referrer can't be removed or changed |
