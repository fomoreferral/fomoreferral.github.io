## Definitions

- **Route:** the path Fomo uses to fill a trade. On Solana it sets which fee schedule applies and can change trade to trade.
- **Network fee:** the blockchain's own charge for processing a transaction (gas). Fomo pays it on Solana; elsewhere the user pays it on top of Fomo's fee, shown in the quote.
- **Token rent:** a small Solana charge for keeping a token account on-chain. Fomo pays it.
- **Notional:** the full size of a leveraged perps position, not just the margin put in. Hyperliquid's fee is charged on it.
